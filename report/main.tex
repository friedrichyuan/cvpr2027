\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{Open-AoE}, an open-source release of 2,000 hours of egocentric manipulation data collected entirely from consumer smartphones, accompanied by a complete data-to-model toolchain. The dataset features bilingual (Chinese-English) atomic action annotations, MANO hand pose reconstructions, and camera trajectories. Our toolchain comprises three components: (1)~\textit{AoE-Visualization}, a Rerun-based data browser; (2)~\textit{AoE-Retarget-Replay}, enabling human-to-robot motion transfer with real-hardware replay; and (3)~\textit{AoE-Training-Ready}, providing format converters and training recipes for mainstream models including $\pi_{0.5}$, GR00T, and fastWAM. Experiments demonstrate that augmenting robot teleoperation data with Open-AoE significantly improves manipulation policy performance.
