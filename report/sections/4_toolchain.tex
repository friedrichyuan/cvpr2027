\section{Open-AoE Toolchain}

Open-AoE provides three complementary tools that collectively bridge the gap from raw egocentric data to trained robot policies.

\subsection{AoE-Visualization}

We develop a Rerun-based data browser that enables intuitive exploration of the Open-AoE dataset. The tool provides synchronized visualization of: (1) egocentric video with overlaid MANO hand meshes; (2) 3D camera trajectory in world coordinates; and (3) an interactive timeline of atomic action annotations with bilingual captions. This allows researchers to rapidly assess data quality, filter segments by criteria, and compare across scenarios.

\subsection{AoE-Retarget-Replay}

This component converts AoE human demonstrations into executable robot joint trajectories, validated through real-hardware replay. Our pipeline adopts a layered architecture:

\textbf{Layer 1 --- Data Preprocessing}: MANO hand parameters and camera trajectories are parsed from the AoE NPZ format and converted into a unified representation.

\textbf{Layer 2 --- Kinematic Retargeting}: Arm retargeting maps camera-derived body poses to the Unitree G1's 7-DoF arm joints. Hand retargeting uses optimization-based methods~\cite{dexretarget} to map MANO finger keypoints to the Inspire 5-fingered hand's 6-DoF per hand, yielding a 28D joint space: $[\text{L\_ARM}(7), \text{R\_ARM}(7), \text{L\_HAND}(6), \text{R\_HAND}(6), \text{PAD}(2)]$.

\textbf{Layer 3 --- Verification}: Retargeted trajectories are validated in MuJoCo simulation via a 2$\times$2 visualization (ego video, MANO overlay, MuJoCo rendering, and joint trajectory plot).

\textbf{Layer 4 --- Real-Hardware Replay}: Validated trajectories are executed on the Unitree G1 + Inspire hands via the real-time control SDK, demonstrating the physical feasibility of the retargeted motions.

\subsection{AoE-Training-Ready}

This component provides format converters and training recipes that enable direct model training on Open-AoE data. The architecture centers on LeRobot v2.1~\cite{lerobot2024} as the data format hub:

\textbf{Format Conversion}: \texttt{retarget\_npz\_to\_lerobot.py} converts AoE's retargeted NPZ files (28D joint space + egocentric images + bilingual captions) into the LeRobot v2.1 dataset format (Parquet metadata + MP4 video chunks).

\textbf{Model-Specific Adapters}: From the LeRobot hub format, we provide adapters for:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{fastWAM}~\cite{fastwam2025}: Validated training recipe (multihead architecture, Step 100, Loss 0.523).
    \item \textbf{GR00T N1.5/N1.7}~\cite{groot2025}: Isaac-GR00T format adapter, leveraging the FLARE~\cite{flare2025} cross-embodiment fine-tuning framework. Previously validated: Close Laptop SR 45\%$\to$95\% with 50 teleop + 200 AoE episodes.
    \item \textbf{$\pi_{0.5}$ (OpenPI)}~\cite{pi05}: LeRobot-to-OpenPI adapter with flow-matching fine-tuning configuration.
\end{itemize}

Each recipe includes the conversion script, training configuration, and a quickstart guide, enabling researchers to go from downloading Open-AoE data to launching model training within minutes.
